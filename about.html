Our Story

One plumber in 2011. Four today. Same standard.

David Thompson founded this business as a one-man operation serving residential clients in Johannesburg. Fifteen years on, the team has grown — the promise hasn't changed.

History

Built one referral at a time

Thompson & Sons Plumbing was founded in 2011 by David Thompson, a qualified plumber who started out serving homeowners across Johannesburg on his own. Word of mouth carried the business through its first five years — the team now handles installation, maintenance and emergency repair work for homeowners and small commercial clients across the greater Johannesburg area.

We provide reliable, honest and affordable plumbing services with a focus on customer satisfaction and long-term relationships — not one-off jobs.
